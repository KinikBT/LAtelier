# Living Atelier | Luxury European Bathroom & Kitchen Showroom in Phnom Penh

**Living Atelier** (`livingatelier.asia`) is Cambodia’s premier architectural destination for luxury European sanitaryware, bespoke bathroom fixtures, and contemporary kitchen solutions. Located in the heart of Phnom Penh, our 3,000+ sq. ft. flagship showroom curates world-renowned European design for discerning homeowners, architects, interior designers, and luxury property developers across Cambodia.

Living Atelier brings over two decades of regional project specification expertise, technical engineering support, and authentic manufacturer warranties directly to the Cambodian market.

---

## Curated European Partner Brands

Living Atelier is an authorized distributor and showroom partner for leading European and international bathroom and kitchen manufacturers:

* **Hansgrohe & AXOR** — German precision-engineered faucets, overhead rain showers, thermostats, and designer bathroom collections.
* **Duravit** — Iconic designer ceramics, freestanding bathtubs, wall-hung toilets, and bespoke bathroom furniture.
* **Geberit** — Swiss sanitary technology, concealed cistern systems, actuator plates, and advanced architectural drainage solutions.
* **Fima Carlo Frattini** — Contemporary Italian brassware, minimalist mixers, and PVD architectural finishes.
* **Franke** — Swiss kitchen systems, luxury granite and stainless steel sinks, and high-performance kitchen mixers.
* **Elkay** — Commercial and residential bottle filling stations, drinking water systems, and architectural sinks.
* **Apaiser** — Hand-crafted, sustainable luxury stone baths and basins tailored for high-end residential and hospitality projects.
